## Market Entry Strategy — Tom Ford Men's Clothing, Austria

**File:** `tom-ford-market-entry-austria.docx`  
**Type:** Team project (final assessment)  
**Programme:** Business Strategy / Market Research

A comprehensive market entry strategy for Tom Ford's men's clothing line into the Austrian market. The analysis covers market sizing, competitive landscape assessment, consumer behaviour profiling, entry mode selection, and go-to-market recommendations — grounded in primary and secondary research frameworks.

**Skills:** Market research, competitive analysis, market sizing, consumer insights, strategic frameworks, entry mode strategy, go-to-market planning
