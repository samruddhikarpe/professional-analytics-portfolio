## Starbucks Balanced Scorecard — Part 1

**File:** `starbucks-balanced-scorecard-part1.docx`  
**Programme:** From Data to KPI

Initial design of a Balanced Scorecard framework for Starbucks across the four BSC perspectives: Financial, Customer, Internal Business Processes, and Learning & Growth. Identifies strategic objectives and maps them to measurable KPIs.

**Skills:** Balanced Scorecard, KPI design, strategic objective mapping, performance measurement
